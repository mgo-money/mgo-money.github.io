Privacy Policy

MGO Robot YouTube · Last updated: October 13, 2026

1. Who uses this app

MGO Robot YouTube is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels MGO Money and MGO Finans, to upload videos to those channels. It is not offered to other people.

2. Data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit permission through Google sign-in, the app requests two YouTube permissions: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and reading basic channel information such as the channel name (youtube.readonly). The app does not read emails, contacts, files or any other Google data.

3. How data is used

The permission is used only to upload the owner's own videos with their titles, descriptions and tags, and to confirm which channel is connected.

4. Storage and sharing

The access permission (token) is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or sent to any other server. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. YouTube's terms apply: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
